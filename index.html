About the cutoff

The screening cutoff is 0.30 (30%). A predicted probability at or above 30% is labelled screen-positive; below 30% is screen-negative.

Sensitivity91.8%
Specificity41.2%

Sensitivity: Among 100 people who truly have CAC >0, about 92 are classified screen-positive; about 8 are missed.

Specificity: Among 100 people who have CAC = 0, about 41 are classified screen-negative; about 59 are classified screen-positive.

Screen-positive means the model meets the study's CAC-testing threshold. Screen-negative does not exclude CAC. Classification uses the unrounded probability.

These operating characteristics come from the development cohort of 637 participants using saved final predictions: 315/343 CAC-positive and 121/294 CAC-zero participants correctly classified. They are not bootstrap optimism-corrected sensitivity or specificity, and external validation has not been established.

How the calculation works

The three laboratory entries form one predictor, TyG/HDL-C. The remaining six predictors are age, sex, diabetes, hypertension, statin use and ever-smoking.

TyG = ln[(triglycerides × 88.57)
       × (fasting glucose × 18.0) / 2]
TyG/HDL-C = TyG / HDL cholesterol

All entered laboratory values: mmol/L.
ln is the natural logarithm.

The final coefficients already incorporate shrinkage. The intercept was re-estimated after shrinkage.

LP = −5.77953517693469
   + 0.0717906562148086 × age
   + 1.00765579480896 × male
   + 0.987774903184474 × diabetes
   + 0.529152983356691 × hypertension
   + 0.719526959800929 × statin use
   + 0.532258912367278 × ever smoker
   + 0.116757585701729 × TyG/HDL-C

Probability = 1 / [1 + exp(−LP)]

Male = 1 and female = 0; yes = 1 and no = 0. Ever-smoking includes current and former smoking. Every answer must be provided explicitly. Missing predictors are never replaced with zero or imputed.

Age entries are limited to 18–120 years as an input check; this does not define a validated age range. Laboratory values must be finite and greater than zero, and derived TyG/HDL-C must be positive. Results depend on entering the specified units.
